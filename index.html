Brief

Giancarlo Pastor is a research and innovation professional with more than 15 years of experience across higher education, innovation and entrepreneurship. His career spans universities, research organisations, consulting, industry-led projects and public funding agencies, with a recurring technology focus on artificial intelligence.

He currently works at the University of Helsinki, developing large-scale collaborative initiatives connecting research organisations and technology companies across Europe. He is also Adjunct Professor of Entrepreneurship at IE Business School, where he teaches deep-tech entrepreneurship and EU funding for technology startups.

He also advises a European Commission-funded 6G initiative on AI law and ethics and has served as evaluator, reviewer or expert for 10+ funding organisations. His academic and scientific track record includes 30+ journal articles, conference papers and technical reports, and 20+ journal and conference reviewing roles.

Expertise

01

Funding Roadmap

For SMEs and scale-ups

A multi-year public-funding roadmap linking technology development and growth milestones with relevant European and national instruments. The roadmap maps suitable programmes, including Horizon Europe, EIC, Chips JU and the European Defence Fund; sequences funding opportunities over time; defines project concepts and expected maturity progression; identifies required consortium structures, partner profiles, roles and dependencies; and aligns funding decisions with product, commercialization and scale-up milestones. The output is a structured funding architecture showing which instruments are relevant at each development stage, what capabilities and partnerships are needed, when applications should be prepared and how successive projects can build on one another rather than being pursued as isolated funding opportunities.

02

Pilot Design

For large enterprises

A structured pilot blueprint for testing technology against a defined business or technical challenge. The design covers use-case definition, problem framing, technical and operational requirements, data requirements and availability, stakeholder roles and interfaces, partner profiles, implementation architecture, governance, KPIs, validation criteria, timelines and key risks. Particular attention is given to ensuring that the pilot generates measurable evidence for subsequent adoption, procurement, investment or scale-up rather than remaining an isolated demonstration. Where appropriate, the pilot can be structured to support external R&I funding. The final blueprint specifies what is tested, which data and infrastructure are required, who is involved, how performance is evaluated and what evidence is needed for the next deployment stage.

03

AI Ethics Assessment

For AI projects and programmes

An independent assessment framework for ethical, legal and governance risks in AI-related research, innovation and technology programmes. The assessment covers transparency, human oversight, fairness, accountability, privacy and data use, societal impacts, risk mitigation and alignment with applicable European requirements and project commitments. It is designed as a lifecycle control mechanism rather than a final compliance check, identifying relevant risks, documenting their significance and defining proportionate mitigation measures across development and deployment stages. Deliverables can include periodic ethics reviews, risk registers, governance recommendations, use-case assessments and ethics reporting, providing project or programme management with a structured basis for monitoring responsible AI development and addressing issues before they become implementation or compliance risks.
